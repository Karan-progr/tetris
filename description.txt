Nostalgia hit me hard this weekend. 🎮

I remembered playing Tetris on an old CRT TV back in 3rd grade and thought, "Why not recreate it?"

From the outside, Tetris looks straightforward. I figured it would be a quick 3–4 hour weekend project. Under the hood it's entirely different story.

I ended up rewriting the core logic twice from scratch due to poor early design choices. It was a solid reminder that modularity isn't optional once complexity scales. Properly decoupling grid management, piece rotation, collision detection, and game loops was quite challenging.

A fun weekend build with some great architecture takeaways!
